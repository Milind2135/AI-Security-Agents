# Third-party intake form (business owner)

Fill in one form per **engagement** (one way your team will use this vendor). If you don't know an answer, write `Don't know` — the agent then treats that factor as **High** until someone confirms it, so it is better to find out.

Save the filled form as `docs/vendors/<vendor>/intake-<engagement>.md` (that folder is private and never pushed to GitHub). Do not paste passwords, keys or real personal data into this form.
